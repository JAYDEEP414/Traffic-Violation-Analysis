# 🚦 City Traffic Violation Analytics (India)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes traffic violation data across major Indian cities using SQL and Power BI. The goal is to uncover patterns in violations, evaluate revenue collection efficiency, and provide actionable insights for traffic management and policy improvement.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze total traffic violations and revenue generation
* Evaluate fine collection efficiency and recovery rate
* Identify high-risk cities and violation hotspots
* Understand vehicle-wise and time-based violation trends
* Analyze repeat offender behavior
* Study the impact of weather conditions on violations

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Description

The dataset contains traffic violation records with key attributes such as:

* Violation ID
* City and Area
* Vehicle Type
* Violation Type
* Date & Time (Hour, Month)
* Fine Amount
* Payment Status (Paid / Unpaid)
* Weather Condition
* Repeat Offender Indicator

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* SQL (Data querying and transformation)
* Power BI (Dashboard and visualization)
* CSV Dataset

## 📈 Key Analysis Performed

### 🔹 Violation Distribution

### 🔹 Revenue Analysis

### 🔹 Behavior Analysis

* Repeat vs non-repeat offenders

### 🔹 External Factors

* Impact of weather conditions on traffic violations

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* Traffic violations are evenly distributed across major cities, each contributing around 10%.
* Approximately 70% of fines are successfully collected, indicating moderate recovery efficiency.
* Bikes and cars contribute the highest number of violations.
* A significant portion (~30%) of fines remain unpaid, leading to revenue loss.
* Most offenders are non-repeat, suggesting enforcement impacts behavior.
* Peak violation hours align with traffic congestion periods.
* Weather conditions show noticeable influence on violation frequency.
